# Design Direction - Draft

A cinematic coastal observatory converted into a believable game-development studio: realistic scale, restrained materials, cool exterior light, warm interior light, and composed exhibit views.

Each exhibit has two layers:

1. **Experience:** finished gameplay, model, animation, or project result.
2. **Breakdown:** systems, debug views, topology, rig, graph, or problem-solving explanation.

First vertical slice: entrance, direct project navigation, one complete Prop Hunt exhibit, transformation breakdown, and reliable return.

Keep travel short. Video remains primary evidence. Avoid excessive bloom, particles, camera shake, head bob, and compulsory introductions. Provide touch, reduced-motion, and non-3D paths.

Status: draft. Do not implement during the current planning task.
