1. PROP TRANSFORMATION AND PIVOT HANDLING
Evidence: BP_propChar contains IA_ChangeProp -> ChangePropOnServer; sphere trace; static-mesh actor cast; tag/allowed-mesh checks; UpdateMeshMulticast and Set Static Mesh. PropChangesLeft is replicated; MaxPropChanges defaults to 10. MeshWithFAlsePivot selects between fixed relative-location adjustments.
Record: transform into three differently sized props, show the same change from a second player's view, then show rotation lock. Capture a side view of a normal prop and a prop using the pivot adjustment.
Breakdown: annotate selection, eligibility, server request, mesh update, and pivot correction.
Accurate framing: mesh selection and pivot-specific corrections. The inspected graph does not substantiate the dossier's general dynamic capsule-resizing formula or collision-safe placement claim.

2. DECOY PROPS
Evidence: Spawnproponserver, SpawnActor BP_SPawnedProp, MulticastSetMesh; replicated SpawnPropsLeft with MaxSpawnProps defaulting to 5. BP_SPawnedProp includes physics and gravity setup.
Record: spawn a decoy, move away, and show a hunter interacting with it. Show the limit being reached if the current build supports that reliably.
Breakdown: reuse the current prop mesh, spawn the actor, assign its visual representation, consume the available count.
Validation needed: verify destruction on both host and remote client. A hunter event named Server_DestroyFakeProp is actually configured as an owning-client RPC; names alone do not establish server authority.

3. HUNTER SHOOTING, DAMAGE, AND SPECTATING
Evidence: StartFireOnServer drives a looping 0.1-second timer; StopFireOnServer clears it. A client-camera request feeds LineTraceOnserver and F_RifleLinerace. The rifle graph summary contains line tracing, damage, hit effects, and crosshair feedback. BP_propChar handles AnyDamage behind an authority check, health updates, hiding/collision disable, and spectator spawning/possession.
Record: shoot a real prop, show feedback and health loss, then show elimination from both players' views and the eliminated player's spectator view.
Breakdown: input -> firing timer -> aim information -> trace/damage -> presentation -> death/spectator transition.
Do not claim lag compensation, rewind buffers, anti-cheat validation, or measured latency performance from this inspection.

4. TAUNTS AND VOICE CHAT
Evidence: reliable server and multicast taunt events, a sound array, Spawn Sound at Location, a one-second cooldown delay, and an automatic-taunt timer. The hunter has VOIPTalker registration, replicated VoiceMode, voice settings, and voice start/stop calls. C++ UVOIPLIB::ClearVoicePackets calls the online voice interface's ClearVoicePackets.
Record: a taunt from two players' perspectives; then voice mode changes with another player speaking. Include captions and label the active mode.
Breakdown: positional clue, cooldown, voice-state propagation, and voice cleanup.
Do not claim verified occlusion, specific falloff curves, or flawless network audio without inspecting settings and recording a playtest.

5. ROUND FLOW AND MULTIPLAYER PRESENTATION
Evidence: GM_ProphuntGameMode tracks players, hunter/prop groups, SeekerCount, alive props, round timers, win-screen calls, and travel. BP_ThirdPersonPlayerController bridges gameplay events to client HUD, countdown, health, kill feed, role setup, and player list. PlayerState01 replicates player name and voice mode.
Record: two-player entry, role assignment, countdown, elimination/round result, and the next round. Include the player list and kill feed.
Validation needed: inspect which role-selection branches are active. GameMode exists on the server, so multicast labels on GameMode events alone do not prove delivery to clients; confirm the downstream controller calls and actual client presentation.

6. ANIMATION AND CHARACTER CUSTOMISATION
Evidence: hunter graph has server/multicast emotes, montage selection, cosmetic mesh switching, and construction-script leader-pose setup. Retargeting and animation assets exist, but their rig configuration was not deeply inspected in this pass.
Record: locomotion, rifle action, two emotes, and a cosmetic change from another player's view. Add a rig/retargeting editor clip after its setup is verified.
Credit imported characters and animation sources. Confirm which models, rigs, materials, and animations you authored before presenting them as original work.
